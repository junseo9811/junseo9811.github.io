LabFinder

Spring 2026

Career-track-based SNU lab recommendation system · Computer Programming, Seoul National University

LabFinder is a command-line Java application that helps students compare research laboratories through the lens of their preferred career path.

  • Built separate recommendation strategies for Big Tech, industry, startup, and PhD pathways using the Strategy Pattern and interface-based extension.
  • Designed multi-dimensional matching that normalizes and weights publication record, overseas placement, career distribution, time-to-graduation, and user priorities such as papers, salary, work-life balance, and network.
  • Implemented persistent student and lab profiles with recommend, search, compare, bookmark, and profile-management commands across 35 SNU lab records.
